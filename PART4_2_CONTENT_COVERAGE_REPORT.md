## 3. Constitutional Mapping Studio Implementation

The Editorial Workspace ([`src/components/admin/EditorialWorkspace.tsx`](file:///g:/Development/insafarchive/src/components/admin/EditorialWorkspace.tsx)) was extended to add a dedicated **Constitutional Mapping Studio** operating alongside the Record Draft Builder.

### Form Fields & Capabilities

1. **Target Archive Record Selector**:
   * Dropdown populated from `ALL_ARCHIVE_RECORDS` with ID, title, and formatted date.
2. **Constitutional Article & Clause Selector**:
   * Selection from all 104 active articles; clauses dropdown dynamically populates based on selected Article.
3. **9-Tier Relationship Taxonomy**:
   * `potential_constitutional_concern`
   * `constitutional_question`
   * `alleged_violation`
   * `officially_disputed`
   * `court_examined`
   * `court_found_violation`
   * `court_rejected_violation_claim`
   * `constitutional_compliance`
   * `historical_constitutional_context`
4. **4-Tier Alert Badge System**:
   * RED: Serious Potential Concern / Allegation
   * ORANGE: Concern Under Review
   * BLUE: Legal Dispute / Historical Context
   * GREEN: Final Judicial Determination
5. **Bilingual Factual & Legal Nexus**:
   * Separate English and Urdu input fields for factual occurrence and constitutional reasoning.
6. **Court Ruling Summary Fields**:
   * Court Name (English/Urdu), Case Citation, Finding Outcome, Operative Ruling Text.
7. **Evidence & Source Linkage**:
   * Supporting Claim IDs, Source Citation IDs, and Applicable Start/End Dates.
8. **Legal Review Status Control**:
   * `suggested` (unreviewed suggestion), `under_review`, `approved`, `rejected`.
9. **Export Modalities**:
   * Single-click **Download Mapping JSON** bundle.
   * Single-click **Copy GitHub Mapping Issue / PR Template** for transparent community review on GitHub Pages without server-side tokens.

## 6. Archival Epistemology & Neutrality Affirmation

In compliance with the project principles:
1. **No AI-generated text has been labeled as official statutory language**.
2. **Every active article is tied to canonical primary government sources** (National Assembly Gazette, Pakistan Code, or Senate Extraordinary Gazettes).
3. **No factual incident is labeled a constitutional violation without citing an operative judgment** of a competent court.
4. **Historical integrity is preserved**: Omitted articles (e.g. Article 247 on FATA) and repealed authoritarian clauses (such as 58(2)(b)) are preserved with their amendment provenance rather than silently erased.
