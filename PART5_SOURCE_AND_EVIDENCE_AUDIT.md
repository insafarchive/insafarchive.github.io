## 1. Evidentiary Standards & Typology

Insaf Archive enforces strict separation between factual findings, official declarations, and unadjudicated allegations. Across all dossiers and records, every claim is tagged with an immutable evidentiary finding type:

| Finding Type | Definition | Verification Threshold | Example in Archive |
|---|---|---|---|
| `court_finding` | Operative ruling, ratio decidendi, or binding directive issued by an apex or superior court | Primary certified judgment (PLD / SCMR / High Court Order Sheet) | SC declaration that civilian military trials violate fundamental rights (`clm-mil-trial-unconst`) |
| `commission_finding` | Factual conclusion reached by a statutory or judicial inquiry commission | Published, declassified commission inquiry report | Hamoodur Rahman Commission conclusion on command breakdown (`clm-hamoodur-political-failure`) |
| `audit_finding` | Quantified accounting or fiscal metric determined by an official state auditor or regulatory committee | Official audit report, NEPRA tariff determination, or parliamentary auditor review | 2020 Power Sector Inquiry calculation of capacity payments (`clm-ipp-capacity-quantum`) |
| `official_position` | Formal press release, cabinet decision, parliamentary speech, or departmental defense | Official gazette, Hansard/parliamentary record, or verified departmental communiqué | ISPR 10 May 2023 press statement regarding installation breaches (`clm-may09-official-ispr-statement`) |
| `documented_fact` | Verified physical, geographical, or historical occurrence not subject to factual dispute | Independent multi-source corroboration or official international registry | EASA official safety suspension notice for PIA (`clm-easa-suspension-order`) |
| `unresolved_question` | Material point of factual or legal contention lacking verifiable primary source resolution | Documented archival gap or absence of published forensic records | Unreleased post-mortem ballistics data in Muridke 2021 clashes (`clm-muridke-civilian-claim`) |

## 3. Epistemological Integrity Audit Findings

The automated test suite (`src/test/dossiers.test.ts`) verified that:
1. **Zero Unsupported Court Findings:** Every single claim categorized as `court_finding` cites an authentic judicial source (`court_judgment`, `court_order`, `supreme_court_record`, or `high_court_record`). No news article or social media post is used to substantiate a court finding.
2. **Zero Dangling Citations:** All 12 dossiers have 100% referential integrity between chronology items, claim source IDs, and the bibliography array in `dossier.sources`.
3. **Draft Quarantine:** Controversial events lacking primary judicial inquiries (e.g., Muridke 2021) are quarantined as `draft` and `under_investigation`, preventing premature publication.
