## 1. Overview of Historical Versioning Model

The Constitution of Pakistan (1973) has undergone extensive structural modification through 26 numbered amendment Bills. 

A critical finding of this audit is that **cataloging an amendment does not mean historical versions have been automatically versioned in full statutory text**. To prevent false claims of completeness, this audit distinguishes:

1. **Enacted Constitutional Amendment Acts**: Formally passed by two-thirds majorities in both Houses of Parliament and gazetted.
2. **Lapsed / Withdrawn Amendment Bills**: Bills introduced in Parliament but never enacted (e.g., 9th, 11th, 15th Amendments).
3. **Preserved Pre-Amendment Text**: Specific provisions where previous statutory text is preserved alongside the consolidated text (e.g., Article 58(2)(b), Article 6, Article 247).
4. **Commencement vs. Assent Discrepancies**: Provisions where the effective date of an amendment differs from Presidential assent.

## 3. Preservation of Omitted & Repealed Provisions

A key rule of archival epistemology is that **repealed or omitted constitutional provisions must not be silently deleted**. In the Insaf Archive:

1. **Article 58(2)(b)**: The authoritarian clause allowing the President to dissolve the National Assembly at discretion (inserted by 8th Amendment 1985, repealed by 13th Amendment 1997, revived by 17th Amendment 2003, and permanently repealed by 18th Amendment 2010) is preserved in `PK-CONST-A058` with full amendment provenance.
2. **Article 247 (Administration of Tribal Areas)**: Omitted by the 25th Amendment 2018 to dismantle the colonial Frontier Crimes Regulation (FCR) system and merge FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Retained in `PK-CONST-A247` with status `'omitted'`.
3. **Article 270AA**: Enacted by the 18th Amendment to declare General Pervez Musharraf's 1999 military takeover and 2007 emergency proclamation *void ab initio*. Fully preserved in `PK-CONST-A270AA`.
4. **Articles 71, 96, 134, 135, 152A, 203I, 212A, 212B**: Cataloged in the Coverage Register with exact repeal/omission annotations.
