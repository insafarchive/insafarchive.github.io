## 1. Statutory Provenance & Archival Policy

Every provision incorporated into the **Insaf Archive** must be traceable to authentic legislative enactments published by the Parliament of Pakistan or the Ministry of Law and Justice.

### Strict Rules of Acquisition & Verification:
1. **Primary Legislative English**: The authoritative statutory English text published in the official Gazette of Pakistan represents the benchmark statutory wording.
2. **Official Urdu Statutory Text**: Only Urdu text published in the official Urdu version by the National Assembly Secretariat (`SRC-CONST-NA-UR-2022`) or official Gazette notifications is designated as `official_verified`.
3. **Editorial Urdu Translation**: In instances where official government Gazette Urdu text is not yet digitally verified or cross-referenced, translations are prepared by archival legal editors, clearly tagged as `editorial_translation`, and distinguished from primary statutory text. Under no circumstances is an editorial translation labeled official.
4. **No Synthetic / Hallucinated Law**: Statutory text is never generated by AI language models.
5. **No Fabricated URLs**: Only URLs directly inspected and verified on official government domains (`na.gov.pk`, `senate.gov.pk`, `pakistancode.gov.pk`) are recorded.
