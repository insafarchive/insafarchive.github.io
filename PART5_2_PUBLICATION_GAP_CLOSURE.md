# Insaf Archive — Part 5.2: Publication Gap Closure & Dossier Readiness Assessment

## 1. Editorial Directive & Evaluation Protocol

In accordance with Insaf Archive publication safeguards, **a dossier cannot be marked `approved` or `published` merely because its TypeScript data structure is syntactically complete**.

A dossier may only be certified for public archival release when:
1. All material claims are verified against primary statutory, judicial, or gazette sources.
2. Judicial orders are checked for subsequent stay orders or appellate reversals.
3. Official state responses and opposing contentions are documented to maintain archival impartiality.
4. Privacy audits confirm that non-public individuals, victims, or vulnerable witnesses are redacted.
5. All Urdu translations accurately match the English statutory definitions.

## 2. Comprehensive Gap Analysis of the Six Non-Published Dossiers

### Dossier 1: Military Court Trials of Civilians (`military-courts`)
- **Current Status**: `under_review` (Safe retained state; NOT published)
- **Primary Blockers & Gap Inventory**:
  1. **Pending Appellate Review**: The Supreme Court 5-0 judgment of 23 October 2023 (holding Section 2(1)(d) ultra vires) was **stayed** by a 5-1 majority of an Intra-Court Appeal (ICA) bench on 13 December 2023. Publishing this dossier as "trials struck down" without the concluded ICA final judgment misleads the public on the active constitutional reality.
  2. **Missing Primary Documents**: The detailed reasons/signatures of all five original bench members were delivered asynchronously; the ICA bench's final written order on the maintainability of the appeal is awaiting delivery.
  3. **Official Responses Needed**: Ministry of Defence compliance filings and Attorney General statements regarding the release of 20 acquitted civilians in April 2024.
  4. **Privacy Review**: Identity protection for non-commissioned military personnel and civilian detainees whose family members have petitioned High Courts under Article 199.
- **Conditions Required Prior to Publication**:
  - The Supreme Court ICA bench delivers its final binding determination disposing of the appeal.
  - Verification of the physical release of acquitted individuals via verifiable judicial bail/release orders.
  - Full archival ingestion of the final Supreme Court ICA text.

### Dossier 2: 9 May 2023 Events & Subsequent Legal Proceedings (`may-09`)
- **Current Status**: `under_review` (Safe retained state; NOT published)
- **Primary Blockers & Gap Inventory**:
  1. **Unconcluded Criminal Trials**: Over 100 Anti-Terrorism Court (ATC) trials and dozens of High Court post-arrest bail petitions are actively sub-judice.
  2. **Contradictory Official Accounts**: Severe discrepancy between state JIT reports asserting centralized criminal conspiracy versus defense submissions alleging agent provocateurs and spontaneous public reaction.
  3. **Missing Forensic Documents**: Complete Punjab Forensic Science Agency (PFSA) digital video/geolocation verification reports have not been made part of the public court record.
  4. **Incomplete Chronologies**: Post-May 9 custodial disappearances, re-arrests outside prison gates under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order (MPO), and press conferences announcing political resignations require verified court contempt orders.
- **Conditions Required Prior to Publication**:
  - Final adjudication of test cases in ATC courts or conclusive High Court appellate determinations.
  - Ingestion of certified bail judgments analyzing Section 7 ATA applicability.
  - Redaction of minor defendants and private bystanders captured in public surveillance footage.

### Dossier 3: 25–26 May 2022 Azadi March Confrontation (`may-26`)
- **Current Status**: `under_review` (Safe retained state; NOT published)
- **Primary Blockers & Gap Inventory**:
  1. **Contempt Proceedings Status**: The Supreme Court's suo motu / contempt proceedings regarding whether political leaders breached the undertaking to restrict protestors to the H-9 ground remain dormant/unconcluded without a final written finding.
  2. **Missing Judicial Inquest**: No independent judicial inquiry commission was constituted to assess the proportionality of tear gas, shipping container blockades, and live firearm allegations.
  3. **Missing Official Medical Records**: Medico-legal certificates (MLCs) from PIMS and Poly Clinic hospitals verifying injuries of police officers and protestors have not been officially disclosed.
- **Conditions Required Prior to Publication**:
  - Archival verification of the final Supreme Court order disposing of the contempt petition.
  - Inclusion of the Ministry of Interior's official security assessment alongside PTI's legal rejoinders.
  - Ingestion of Punjab and Islamabad Police internal review reports.

### Dossier 6: Muridke GT Road Clashes & Police Fatalities (`muridke-tragedy`)
- **Current Status**: `draft` (Safe retained state; NOT published)
- **Primary Blockers & Gap Inventory**:
  1. **Absence of Independent Judicial Commission**: Unlike Model Town, no judicial tribunal investigated the fatal clashes between Punjab Police and TLP cadres at Sadhoke/Muridke in October 2021.
  2. **Classified Executive Agreement**: The settlement brokered by religious figures and the National Security Council ending the march was never gazetted or tabled before Parliament.
  3. **Contested Casualty Figures**: Severe discrepancy between Punjab Police casualty figures (fatalities due to firearms and vehicle rammings) versus protest organizers' claims of police firing.
  4. **Missing Post-Mortem & Ballistic Reports**: Certified ballistic reports confirming bullet calibers and firing ranges are absent from public archives.
- **Conditions Required Prior to Publication**:
  - Availability of declassified official inquiry documents or concluded court judgments in FIRs registered under ATA.
  - Integration of verified Punjab Home Department reports.
  - Complete Urdu terminology normalization to prevent inflammatory characterization.
