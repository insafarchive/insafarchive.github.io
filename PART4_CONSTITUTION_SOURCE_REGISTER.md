## 1. Statutory Grounding & Archival Provenance Policy

The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan was passed by the National Assembly on 10 April 1973, received Presidential assent on 12 April 1973, and commenced on 14 August 1973.

All constitutional provisions incorporated into the **Insaf Archive** must be traceable to primary legislative publications issued under the authority of the Parliament of Pakistan or the Ministry of Law and Justice.

### Strict Translation & Source Rules
1. **Primary Legislative English**: The English text published in the official Gazette of Pakistan represents the authoritative statutory language passed by Parliament.
2. **Official Urdu Statutory Text**: Where an authentic Urdu version published by the National Assembly of Pakistan or the Ministry of Law and Justice is verified, it is designated as `official_verified`.
3. **Editorial Urdu Translation**: In instances where official government Gazette Urdu text is not yet digitally verified or cross-referenced, translations are prepared by archival legal editors, clearly tagged as `editorial_translation`, marked with an `unverified_statutory` review state, and distinguished from primary statutory text.
4. **Zero Fabrication**: Under no circumstances will fictional URLs, unverified amendments, or speculative enactment dates be entered.

## 5. Verification Protocols & Maintenance Log

1. **Bi-Annual Audit**: Every gazetted amendment and parliamentary enactment is checked against the National Assembly and Senate legislative repositories.
2. **Citation Standard**: All references adhere to standard Pakistani legal citation format (e.g., `PLD [Year] SC [Page]`, `SCMR`, `CLC`, `PCrLJ`, `MLD`).
3. **No External Secret Dependencies**: All canonical texts are mirrored as static TypeScript structures in the repository.
